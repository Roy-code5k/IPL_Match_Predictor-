# Pre-match models (Phase 4)

Generated by `python -m src.models.prematch` from `data/processed/features.csv` (feature version 1.1.0). Prediction moment: after the toss, before the first ball. The target is `team1_win` (Team A wins). The 2023–2024 test seasons were not used.

## Setup

- Data before the test seasons: 946 matches (2008–2022).

- **Walk-forward 2015–2022:** each season is predicted by a model trained on all earlier seasons (489 matches in total).

- **Validation 2022:** trained on 2008–2021 (872 matches), evaluated on 2022 (74 matches).

- Learned models are trained on both team orders and average both orders when predicting.

- **Selection rule (fixed in advance):** lowest walk-forward log loss. A probability of exactly 50% counts as a Team A prediction, so the coin flip's accuracy is Team A's win share.

Lower log loss and Brier score are better. Log loss for an honest 50/50 guess is 0.6931.
